Why Creative Writing Matters

Poetry, short stories, and drama scripts are special because they allow people to express ideas, emotions, experiences, and stories in different creative ways. Poetry uses rhythm, imagery, symbolism, and carefully chosen words to convey deep emotions and thoughts, often in only a few lines. Short stories are special because they can present a complete story within a limited amount of space, focusing on characters, conflicts, and meaningful events that readers can easily connect with. Drama scripts, on the other hand, are written to be performed, allowing stories to come alive through dialogue, acting, movement, and interaction between characters. Although these literary forms are different, they all help us understand human experiences and see the world from different perspectives.
